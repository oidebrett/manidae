
NemoClaw AI Agent Sandbox
=========================

Your NemoClaw deployment includes:
- OpenClaw AI agent gateway (port 18789)
- NemoClaw plugin with blueprints
- OpenShell sandbox with Landlock + seccomp + netns security
- Traefik reverse proxy with automatic HTTPS via Let's Encrypt

Dashboard: https://${NEMOCLAW_SUBDOMAIN}.${NEMOCLAW_DOMAIN}

Messaging channels (if configured):
- Telegram: Send /start to your bot to begin chatting
- Discord: Add the bot to your server and mention it
- Slack: Add the bot to a channel and mention it

To connect to the sandbox:
  nemoclaw ${NEMOCLAW_SUBDOMAIN} connect

To check status:
  nemoclaw ${NEMOCLAW_SUBDOMAIN} status
